## 3. The video series (why each one spreads)

Research basis: completion rate drives TikTok distribution, with roughly 15–34s the favoured
range; text-heavy and POV clips are outperforming polished ads; recurring series train
viewers to come back; most viewing starts with the sound off.
(Sources: [JetFuel 2026 guide](https://jetfuel.agency/short-form-video-optimization-2026/),
[Pulse on paid formats](https://www.pulse.bot/marketing/video/these-2-formats-dominate-on-paid-ads-8806c873-d72f-4979-b311-5341e12cf3bd/),
[iBuildInfluence](https://medium.com/@peter_ibuildinfluence/viral-content-in-2026-what-works-for-creators-ibuildinfluence-blog-5dc248d91f78),
[viral.app: 3.3M videos](https://viral.app/grow/short-form-video-trends).)

| Series | Format | Length | Why it spreads | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Glow-Up** | Before → after wipe of a website, brand or workflow | 15–25s | Transformation payoff at the end keeps people watching to completion | Concept mock-ups (I make them) or approved client work |
| **POV: the client said…** | Text on screen over b-roll or a mascot; relatable agency humour with a real lesson | 10–20s | Relatable, gets tagged and sent to friends | Stock or desk b-roll |
| **Spot the Difference** | Two versions of a screen. "A or B? Comment." Then the reveal | 12–20s | Comment bait with a genuine lesson; comments feed reach | Mock-ups (I make them) |
| **Myth vs Fact** | Fast cards, one myth busted | 15–25s | Saves and shares; positions us as experts | None |
| **Raw vs Edited** | Split screen: raw clip vs our edit | 15–25s | Shows the craft (the video is the portfolio) | A raw clip + our edit |
| **Drop Your Website** | Weekly review of one follower's site (respectful, constructive) | 30–45s | Comment engine; every episode invites new submissions | Submissions (starts Fri 13) |
| **Motion showcase** | Kinetic type / brand film | 10–20s | Pure craft flex; brand recall | Our motion skill |

**Rules for every video:**
- 9:16, with captions burned in.
- Hook text in frame 1.
- No logo intro.
- End card: eagle mark + EAGLESCROFT + one CTA.
- No real people in AI-made realistic scenes.
- Label AI voice or AI visuals where the platform asks.
- Post **TikTok → Shorts → Reels → Facebook Reels → LinkedIn → X**.
- Use 3–5 hashtags, not more.

**Where:** EAGLESCROFT is set up on LinkedIn, Facebook and X. The video series also needs
**Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts** for EAGLESCROFT. Recommended: open them with the same
handle and bios from `profiles/profile-setup.md`.
