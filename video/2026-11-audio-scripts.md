# Joshua · audio scripts · November 2026

Recording-ready voiceovers for all 16 videos in [`2026-11-scripts.md`](2026-11-scripts.md).
Plain-text copies for AI voice tools are in [`audio/`](audio/) (one `.txt` per video).

**Marks:** `…` = short pause (about 0.3s) · `—` = quick beat (about 0.15s) · **bold** = stress
(these are also the words that pop up on screen) · *italics* = quoted speech, so change your tone.

**Every video:**
- Speak at about **3 words a second**. Tool drops run a little faster, stories a little slower.
- Leave **about 1.5s of music** after the last word for the end card.
- Say the comment keyword slightly **slower and louder**.
- **AI voice:** paste the text from `audio/`, keep the `…` marks, and turn on the AI-content label when posting.
- **Your own voice:** record in a quiet room or a wardrobe full of clothes, with the phone about 15 cm away.
