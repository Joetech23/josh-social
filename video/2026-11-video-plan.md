# Joshua · faceless motion videos · November 2026 plan

Four videos a week for Joshua's personal accounts, 2–29 November 2026 (16 videos). Faceless,
voiceover-led motion graphics in the style of the three reference videos. Topics: tech, AI and
the everyday-AI themes from the graphics calendar (traders, students, small business, building
with AI, African tech). Every script is in [`2026-11-scripts.md`](2026-11-scripts.md).

## 1. What the reference videos do

I transcribed and broke down all three samples. Each one is a repeatable format.

| Sample | Length | Format | Hook pattern | Ending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "3 dangerous websites" | 22s | **Drop**: 3 resources, one line each | Audience call-out + forbidden knowledge + open loop: *"Video editors. I'm about to share three dangerous websites your favourite editors don't want you to know. The last one might be the best."* | *"Want the links? Drop **Brilliant** in the comments and check your DMs."* |
| LEGO origin | 40s | **Story**: rise from nothing | Before/after contrast: *"Before LEGO became a billion-dollar empire, it was just a struggling wooden toy business."* | Lesson + soft plug |
| After Effects grid plugin | 95s | **How-to**: tool demo, step by step | Speed + no-pain promise: *"This is how you create this trendy animation in under a minute. And you don't need multiple layers, keyframes or expressions."* | *"Send it to an editor who needs it. Comment **grid** and we'll send you the plugin for free."* |
| Press-conference still | — | **News scene**: realistic AI-rendered scene | Event framing | — |

Shared mechanics:
- **Pace:** about 3 words a second. Speech never pauses for more than half a second.
- **Kinetic type:** one to three words on screen at a time, in sync with the voice.
- **One hero visual per beat:** a browser window, a 3D diorama, a UI panel or a mascot.
- **The keyword is an object:** the comment keyword appears as a styled button, e.g. "Brilliant".
- **The ending asks for one action:** comment a word, send it to someone, or follow.

## 2. What the research says (and how sure we can be)

| Finding | Confidence | What we do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, named **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** as the top Reels ranking signals (Jan 2025). Sends matter most for reaching people who don't follow you yet. | High (his own statement, widely reported) | Every script is short and keeps paying off to hold watch time. Each week has at least one **"send this to…"** video (Sun explainers, Wed tutorials). |
| Most viewers decide in the first **1–3 seconds**. Published hold rates conflict (60% to 90% at 3s), and **faceless openings are harder** because there's no face to hold attention. | Medium (vendor data, conflicting) | The first hook line lands within 3 seconds, with movement and text in frame 1. No logos or slow intros. Judge by your own retention curves, not benchmarks. |
| **Comment-keyword → auto-DM** is well supported on **Instagram/Facebook** (ManyChat and other official Meta partners). On **TikTok**, comment-triggered DMs are unreliable in 2026. | High for Instagram; mixed for TikTok | Instagram/Facebook: automate it. TikTok: same CTA, but reply by hand or pin a comment pointing to the link in bio. Check ManyChat's pricing first: reports say its free plan changed in March 2026. |
| **AI labels:** TikTok, YouTube and Meta all require a label on **realistic** AI-made people, places, events or voices. TikTok reads embedded C2PA data and can label automatically. Unlabelled realistic AI can be restricted. | High | Turn on the AI-content toggle whenever a video uses an AI voice or realistic AI scenes. **No realistic AI depictions of real people** (founders, politicians). Stories use objects, places and stylised figures. |
| No reliable study gives a Lagos/WAT posting time. One Nigeria guide suggests TikTok around **7–8 pm WAT**. Global Reels data is inconsistent. | Low | Start at **7:30 pm WAT**, then move to the peak hours in your own analytics after two weeks. |

## 3. The four weekly formats

| Day | Format | Length | Look | Job in the funnel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mon** | **Tool Drop**: 3 free tools | 28–33s | **A · Navy Drop** (based on the "dangerous websites" sample) | Comments + saves → DM list |
| **Wed** | **How-To in 60**: one useful workflow | 45–60s | **C · Grid Desk** (based on the plugin sample) | Saves + sends → trust |
| **Fri** | **Origin Story**: a tech company's near-miss | 45–55s | **B · Diorama** (based on the LEGO sample) | Watch time + follows |
| **Sun** | **AI Explained**: one idea in plain English | 40–47s | **D · Newsroom** (based on the press scene, made safe) | Sends + reach to new people |

**Posting:** 7:30 pm WAT. Post natively to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.
Add LinkedIn (native video) for Mon, Wed and Sun. Don't cross-post with another app's watermark.

### Visual looks (one identity across all four)

Use Joshua's personal palette so the videos match his graphics: deep navy `#001E60` → blue
`#2563EB` fields, white type, **Sora** headlines with a **Caveat** handwritten accent, and the
flight marker as the bullet. Swap the reference video's gold for blue light leaks.

- **A · Navy Drop:** dark navy gradient with a soft blue light leak. Real screen recordings sit
  in floating browser windows that slide in. Word-by-word kinetic type. The comment keyword is
  an outlined button at the end.
- **B · Diorama:** stylised 3D miniature scenes (a garage, a Denny's booth, a Nairobi kiosk).
  Big bold 3D year stamps ("1993"). Objects carry the story: chips, phones, documents.
  **Stylised, not photoreal, and no recognisable real faces.**
- **C · Grid Desk:** green or navy cutting-mat grid. Real UI screenshots in floating frames,
  step numbers, cursor highlights. Use **an original mascot of your own design**, not a
  lookalike of a film character (the sample used one; that's a copyright risk).
- **D · Newsroom:** the press-conference energy, made safe: real stock or archive footage, or
  **stylised** AI scenes with no real people, plus headline lower-thirds and data cards.
  Never show a real person saying something they didn't say.

### Production specs

1080×1920 at 30 fps. Burn in captions, because many people watch with the sound off. Keep text
out of the bottom 20% and the right 15% (the platform buttons sit there). The keyword button
holds on screen for at least 1.5 seconds. End card: *"Joshua Obaje · AI made simple"* plus the
ask. Use one consistent voice: your own recorded voice (most authentic) or one AI voice for every
video (label it). Light background music, ducked under the voice.

## 5. DM pack (what the auto-reply sends)

Set each keyword as an Instagram/Facebook comment trigger. Reply text:

- **TOOLS:** "Here you go 👇 NotebookLM: notebooklm.google.com · Gamma: gamma.app · Napkin: napkin.ai. All have free plans with limits. Which one are you trying first?"
- **INVOICE:** "Here's the prompt: *'Turn this into a professional invoice. Business name: [name]. Add an invoice number, today's date, a table with item, quantity, unit price and line total, then the grand total and payment details: [bank details]. Use naira.'* Check the total yourself before sending."
- **STUDY:** "Consensus: consensus.app · Semantic Scholar: semanticscholar.org · Zotero: zotero.org. Always open and read the papers you cite."
- **WHATSAPP:** five messages to copy: greeting, away, price list, delivery, "last price" (written in Joshua's voice, in the scripts file under V06).
- **AGENT:** "Noted 🙌 I'm putting together a step-by-step agent demo. You'll get it here first."
- **DESIGN:** "Photopea: photopea.com · remove.bg · Upscayl: upscayl.org (free desktop app)."
- **VOICE:** "Paste 3 things you wrote, then: *'Study my writing. Describe my tone, sentence length, favourite words and words I never use, as rules another writer could follow.'* Save the result in ChatGPT custom instructions, a Claude Project or a Gemini Gem."
- **ADMIN:** "Tally: tally.so · Perplexity: perplexity.ai · Cal.com: cal.com."
- **WEEK:** the full prompt from V14.

## 6. How we'll measure

Each week, log per video: views, **average watch %**, **3-second hold**, sends/shares, saves,
keyword comments, and follows from the video. After four weeks, double the best format, test new
hooks on the weakest, and plan December.
