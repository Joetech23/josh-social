In 2015, a Nigerian developer was tired of one problem.
Five years later… Stripe bought his company for over two hundred million dollars.

Getting paid online in Nigeria was painful.
Payments failed. Checkout forms were long.
And many tools weren't built for Nigerian cards.

Shola Akinlade co-founded Paystack in Lagos with one idea:
make online payments in Nigeria… just work.

In 2016, it became the first Nigerian company accepted into Y Combinator —
Silicon Valley's most famous startup programme.

Businesses signed up fast… because developers could plug it in easily.
Stripe invested in 2018.
And in October 2020… it bought Paystack — reported at over two hundred million dollars.
