Everyone's saying "AI agents."
Here's what it actually means — in thirty seconds.

A chatbot answers.
An agent acts.

Ask a chatbot for flight prices… and it tells you.
Ask an agent… and it opens the website, compares the options, fills in the form —
and asks: "Shall I book?"

Agents use tools: a browser, your email, your calendar, a spreadsheet.
They break a big task into steps… and work through them.

They're powerful — and they make mistakes.
So the golden rule:
let the agent prepare… and you approve.
Never hand over money or passwords without a human check.
