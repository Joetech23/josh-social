Africa's most famous mobile money service… started as a way to repay small loans.
Then customers did something nobody planned.

Kenya. 2005.
Vodafone and Safaricom, with funding from the UK government, tested a simple idea:
let people repay microloans… by text.

But during the pilot, people started using it for something else.
Sending money to family.
Paying each other.
Topping up airtime.

Instead of forcing the original plan… the team followed the customers.
In March 2007, M-Pesa launched with one main promise — send money.

M… for mobile.
Pesa… Swahili for money.
No bank account needed — just a phone, and an agent around the corner.

Watch what your customers actually do… not what you planned.
