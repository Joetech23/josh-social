On Monday… ChatGPT turns four.
But when it launched, even the people who built it didn't expect what happened next.

November thirtieth, 2022.
OpenAI released ChatGPT as a free research preview.
Basically… a test — to see how people would use it.

Five days later, it had a million users.
Within about two months, an estimated hundred million people were using it every month —
faster than TikTok or Instagram ever grew.

Students used it for homework.
Developers, for code.
Businesses, for emails.
Within months… every major tech company changed its plans.

A product launched as a test…
started the AI race we're all living in now.
