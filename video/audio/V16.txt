If your "brother" calls crying… and asks you to send money now —
stop.
It might not be him.

Scammers can now copy a voice from a short clip — like a video your family posted online.
They call… sound exactly like someone you love… and create panic.

An accident.
An arrest.
A "lost phone."
Panic… is the weapon.

So protect your family today.
One: agree on a family code word — something never posted online.
If the caller can't say it… hang up.
Two: hang up — and call them back on the number you already have.
Three: never send money on a call that rushes you.

Real emergencies can wait two minutes.
Send this to your family group chat — right now.
