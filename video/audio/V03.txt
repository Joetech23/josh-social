Before Nvidia became a five-trillion-dollar company…
it was thirty days from going out of business.

In 1993, three engineers met at a Denny's restaurant in California…
and decided to build chips for 3D graphics.
Jensen Huang became the CEO.

Their first chip — flopped.
By 1997, Nvidia was down to about forty people… and roughly one month of cash.

So they bet everything on one more chip — the RIVA 128.
It sold about a million units in four months.
The company survived.

Then came the twist.
Graphics chips do thousands of small calculations at once.
That's exactly what AI needs.
Today, most of the world's AI runs on Nvidia chips.

Sometimes the thing you build for one market… wins a much bigger one.
