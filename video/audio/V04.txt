ChatGPT just lied to you…
and it didn't even know it.

AI chatbots don't look facts up like Google does.
They predict the next most likely word… one word at a time… from patterns in billions of pages.

Most of the time, the most likely answer is the right one.
But when it doesn't know… the most likely-sounding answer can be completely made up.

So here's the rule.
Use AI to draft, explain and brainstorm.
But anything with a number, a name, a date or a quote — check it yourself.
Or ask for sources… and open them.

Send this to someone who believes everything ChatGPT says.
And follow for AI, explained in plain English.
