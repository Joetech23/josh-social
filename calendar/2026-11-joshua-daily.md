## New video formats (scripts follow each week)

- **AI for… (Tue).** Look C · Grid Desk.
  - Hook: call out the audience by name ("Traders." / "Students." / "Team leads."). Then one problem, one AI fix shown on a phone, and the copyable prompt.
  - CTA: keyword for the prompt (PRICE, QUIZ, POLICY, PROPOSAL).
- **Build Log (Thu).** Faceless. Your real screen, sped up, with marker-style annotations.
  - Script shape: *"Day [x] of building [thing]. Here's what worked… what broke… and what's next."*
  - CTA: "Follow to see if it works."
  - This is the only format that needs your raw material every week.
- **AI This Week (Sat).** Look D · Newsroom. Three verified updates, 15s each:
  - what happened
  - what it means for you
  - CTA: "Follow so you don't miss next Saturday's."

  I research and fact-check the stories every Friday, so nothing stale or unverified goes out.
