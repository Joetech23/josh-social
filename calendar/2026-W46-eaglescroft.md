## Fri 13: Ground School: "Is your website losing customers? The 7-point check"
**Graphic:** `posts/2026-11/013-w46-fri-website-check/` ✅ 6 slides: cover → speed + mobile → clear headline + one action → WhatsApp/call + Google profile → trust signals → CTA.

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Your website might be losing customers quietly. Run this 7-point check today:
>
> 1. Speed: does it load in about 3 seconds on mobile data?
> 2. Mobile: can you read and tap everything without zooming?
> 3. Headline: does a visitor know what you do in 5 seconds?

> 6. Google: is your Business Profile complete, with hours, photos and the website link?
> 7. Trust: padlock (HTTPS), real photos and reviews?
>
> Fewer than 6 ticks? That's where your leads are leaking.

## Mon 16: Ground School: "4 reasons businesses haven't automated yet"
**Graphic:** `posts/2026-11/016-w46-mon-automation-objections/` ✅ 6 slides: cover → "I don't know where to start" → "It sounds expensive" → "My customers want a human" → "I don't trust AI with my data" → CTA.

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> These are the 4 reasons we hear most for not automating yet, and our honest answers:
>
> 1. "I don't know where to start." Start with the question you answer 20 times a day. One workflow, not a whole system.
> 2. "It sounds expensive." Start small with tools you already pay for. Prove the time saved before you spend more.
> 3. "My customers want a human." Good. Automate the waiting, not the relationship: instant replies, then a person takes over.
> 4. "I don't trust AI with my data." Fair. Ask who sees it, where it's stored, and whether it trains the model. Never paste customer data into public tools.
>
> Which one is yours? Tell us below.
> #AIAutomation #SmallBusiness #WorkflowAutomation #Eaglescroft

## Wed 18: Ground School: "Before you boost that post"
**Graphic:** `posts/2026-11/018-w46-wed-ads-checklist/` ✅ 6 slides: cover → who it's for → one goal → the landing spot is ready → tracking on → test small, then scale → CTA.

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Before you press "Boost post", run these 5 checks so your ad money isn't wasted:
>
> 1. Who is it for? Location, age and interests, not "everyone".
> 2. One goal: messages, calls, website visits or sales. Pick one.
> 3. Is the landing spot ready? A fast page or a WhatsApp that someone answers.
> 4. Tracking on: Meta Pixel or Google tag installed, so you know what worked.
> 5. Test small: two versions, a small budget for a few days, then put money behind the winner.
>
> Ads don't fix a broken journey. They make it more expensive.
