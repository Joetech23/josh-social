## Mon 16: Ground School: "5 small UX fixes that make your website easier to buy from"
**Graphic:** `posts/2026-11/020-w46-mon-ux-fixes/` ✅ 7 slides: cover → one main button → ask for less → readable text → buttons for thumbs → show progress → CTA.
**Video:** EV05 Spot the Difference: which form would you fill in?

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Customers don't leave your website because they hate you. They leave because something felt like hard work.
>
> 5 small UX fixes we make on almost every project:
> 1. One main button per screen, and make it look like a button
> 2. Ask for less: every extra field is one more reason to give up
> 3. Text people can actually read: size, contrast, short lines
> 4. Buttons big enough for thumbs, with space between them
> 5. Show progress: "Step 2 of 3", loading states, clear confirmations
>
> Good design feels invisible. Bad design feels like work.
> Want a UX review of your site or app? Link in the first comment.

## Wed 18: Ground School: "Before you boost that post"
**Graphic:** `posts/2026-11/018-w46-wed-ads-checklist/` ✅ 6 slides.
**Video:** EV07 Myth vs Fact: "Search your business on Google right now."

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Before you press "Boost post", run these 5 checks so your ad money isn't wasted:
>
> 1. Who is it for? Location, age and interests, not "everyone".
> 2. One goal: messages, calls, website visits or sales. Pick one.
> 3. Is the landing spot ready? A fast page or a WhatsApp that someone answers.
> 4. Tracking on: Meta Pixel or Google tag installed, so you know what worked.
> 5. Test small: two versions, a small budget for a few days, then put money behind the winner.
>
> Ads don't fix a broken journey. They make it more expensive.
